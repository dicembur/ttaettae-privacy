Frequently asked questions

I'm not getting reminders

  • Check that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings > Notifications > TTAETTAE.
  • In the app's Settings tab, check that Get reminders is on and the reminder time is correct.
  • If you use Do Not Disturb or a Focus mode, reminders may arrive quietly or be held. Sound and Focus follow your device settings.

How do urgent alarms work?

  • When you add or edit an item, turn on Urgent alarm and set a time. On the due date you'll be reminded every minute for 10 minutes from that time, and it repeats every cycle until you turn it off.
  • If Get reminders is off in the Settings tab, urgent alarms won't ring either.
  • Tapping I did it or Tomorrow on an urgent alarm opens the app and stops the remaining alarms.

What happens if I don't sign in?

You can use every feature without signing in. In that case your information stays only on your device, so deleting the app also deletes your information. To switch devices or back up your information, sign in with Apple.

Sync isn't working

Check the sync status in Settings > Account and tap the refresh icon on the right to try again. An internet connection is required.

I want to delete my account and information

Tap Settings > Account > Delete account in the app. Your account and the items, completion records, and categories stored on the server are deleted. For details, see the Privacy Policy.

I want to change the language

In Settings > Language, follow the system setting or choose English, 한국어, 日本語, or 简体中文.
