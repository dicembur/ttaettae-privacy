TTAETTAE Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

TTAETTAE (the "App") handles your information with care. This page explains what the App processes and how.

1. Information the App processes

a. If you use the App without signing in

  • What you enter: item names, categories, cycles, last and next due dates, memos, icons, urgent alarm times, and completion records
  • App settings: language, reminder time, and whether reminders are on

This information is stored only on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to any external server.

b. If you choose to sign in

  • When you sign in with Apple or Google, your account identifier (user ID) and, if your sign-in provider shares it, your email address (a private relay address if you use Apple's Hide My Email) are stored with the authentication service. The App does not show your email on screen.
  • To sync and back up across your devices, the items, completion records, and categories described in (a) are stored on a server.

c. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. The App does not collect a device push token and does not send notifications from a server.

d. Information the App does not collect

The App uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

e. Server logs

When you sign in and sync, running the server may involve ordinary connection logs such as access time and IP address. The App does not separately collect or use them.

2. How the information is used

  • To provide App features such as reminders and records
  • To sign you in and to sync and back up your data
  • To process account deletion

Your information is not used for advertising or marketing, and it is not sold.

3. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or share your information with third parties. To run the App, we use the following services.

  • Supabase Inc. — sign-in authentication and storage of synced data (only if you sign in)
  • Apple Inc. / Google LLC — Sign in with Apple and Google sign-in (each company's own privacy policy applies)

4. International transfer

Information is sent to a server only if you sign in and sync. The server may be located outside your country.

  • Recipient: Supabase Inc.
  • Country: The country where the server is located (it may be outside your country, depending on Supabase's server region)
  • When and how: sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) when you sync
  • Purpose: sign-in and storage of synced data
  • Retention: until you delete your account

5. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: deleted when you delete the App. You can delete items in the App at any time.
  • On the server: kept while your account exists. If you tap Settings > Account > Delete account in the App, your account and the items, completion records, and categories stored on the server are deleted.
  • A deleted item may remain as a "deleted" marker so that your devices stay in sync. It is deleted along with your account.

6. Your rights

You can view, edit, and delete your information directly in the App, and you can delete your account there. For any other request, contact us below.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Security

If you sign in, your information is sent over encrypted connections. On the server, access controls ensure that each user can access only their own information.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page with a new effective date.
